# The locked pick, 2026-08-02, North Country Charters

## What is locked

**The 14-Day Clock, downscoped.** Email only. One classifier node. A two-week
measurement phase in front of it with a real exit.

The ai-feasibility-engineer's recommended pick, taken as the contract requires,
after it downscoped the strategist's version on six counts and killed the
front-desk voice agent outright.

## What the room refused to sell, which is the more useful half

The obvious answer for a Homer charter operator in August is a guest-facing voice
agent on the phone line. The room killed it, at RICE 6.6 against 85, on four
independent grounds, any one of which was sufficient.

- It duplicates a function their existing vendor already ships. FareHarbor's own
  page describes FareHarbor Agent answering guest questions across phone, email,
  chat, SMS and social, checking live availability and pushing a booking link.
  They already pay for FareHarbor.
- The only supporting outcome evidence anywhere in the package is a
  vendor-published case study from an Atlanta car-rental operator. Not
  independent, not tourism.
- The compounding math is fatal. Twenty-five to thirty-five sequential model steps
  on a real booking call puts end-to-end success in the single digits to low tens
  of percent.
- The one peer-reviewed tourism study in the package argues against it on exactly
  this channel. Booking intention fell further with an AI agent than with a human
  in a negative-outcome scenario, and the phone is where weather cancellations and
  refunds land.

Five of the six candidates on the table clear their own bar at the rules tier with
no model in them at all. That fact goes in the study.

## Cagan's four risks, sanity-checked for THIS owner before locking

**Feasibility.** Clear. The engineer walked the ladder. One probabilistic step,
Gmail API read-only, deadline arithmetic in code. Roughly 88 to 93 percent of
money-governed messages alerted at an assumed 90 to 95 percent classifier recall,
against about 53 percent for the six-model-step version originally pitched.

**Usability.** The strongest dimension, and it is a design constraint rather than
a preference. Habit is the largest force against this build, a phone and a person
since 1979. So the build requires zero behaviour change. Nothing new to sign into,
no new number, no new inbox, no dashboard anyone has to remember to open, alerts
to phones they already carry, and an off switch that takes a minute. If it added a
place to look, habit would win and the build would fail.

**Value.** Real but UNMEASURED, and this is the honest weak point of the whole
engagement. The evidence is first-party and in writing, they published a policy
that apologises for the channel and asks customers to route around it. What does
not exist anywhere is a count. No messages per day, no cancellations per season,
no charter price. Value is therefore a hypothesis, and phase one exists to test it
rather than to assume it.

**Business viability for this owner.** The risk that decided the ask. This is a
small seasonal operator, two named owners, one of them a working captain, with a
season that closes mid-September. A build quoted without a count is not defensible
to a business this size, and it is exactly the spend the AI base rate punishes. So
viability forced the ask DOWN to the measurement fortnight rather than the build.

Nothing here drops us to the next candidate. Locked.

## ECONOMICS PRE-CHECK, and the ask this study will make

The rule is the smallest honest ask this build supports, and a pilot whose honest
return is decision data counts. That is precisely what this is.

**THE ASK IS PHASE ONE ONLY. A fixed-fee measurement fortnight, 2,800 dollars.**

What it delivers, in two weeks.
1. Week one, before any model exists, a plain Gmail autoresponder that answers
   every inbound message in the wording already on their own FAQ, asking anyone
   cancelling to call and confirm. Zero AI. It can't be a wrong answer. It is a
   real improvement they keep whatever happens next.
2. The data agreement, provider retention disabled, and their explicit consent.
3. Two weeks of logging and labelling every inbound message, which produces the
   baseline their whole outcome statement needs and which nothing logs today.
4. A written recommendation with a real exit. If the count is small, we say the
   build is not worth making, they keep the autoresponder, and they buy nothing
   further from us.

**Why the conservative case clears, by construction.** The return of this ask is
decision data, and it is priced as decision data. 2,800 dollars buys the number
that decides a build quoted between 5,500 and 8,000, against a base rate where
most AI spend underperforms. If there is even a 40 percent chance the honest
answer is do not build, the fortnight avoids more wrong spend than it costs. The
autoresponder ships regardless and carries value on its own. There is no scenario
in which they pay 2,800 and learn nothing, because the count exists either way.

**What is NOT the ask.** The Clock itself. It is quoted in the study as an
indicative range, explicitly gated on phase one's count, and explicitly not being
asked for today. The ROI section presents it as a BREAK-EVEN THRESHOLD in
recovered seats rather than a dollar return, because no volume and no charter rate
have been verified and a dollar return would be invented.

Pre-check passed. The ask clears its own bar honestly at this size, so Phase 4
runs on the locked pick.

## Binding constraints the engineering room inherits

- Email only in v1. Voicemail is cut, no transcription is evidenced on their line.
- No stamping against the 14-day and 24-hour lines in v1 without a FareHarbor
  read. Days-to-departure can't be derived from an email. v1 surfaces the message
  and the human does the lookup.
- The guest acknowledgement is NOT gated on the classifier. Same canned line to
  every inbound message.
- The classified class is cancellation and no-show risk only. Date and party-size
  changes have no published threshold and are out.
- Strictly additive. Never marks read, never files, never hides. The digest lists
  everything that arrived, not the leftovers.
- Alerts route to the office manager first, escalate to the captain only on
  no-acknowledgement, with a daily cap.
- Month to month, cancellable, no FareHarbor write access, and we stop billing if
  the vendor ships the function.
- We recommend in writing that they request the FareHarbor Agent pilot NOW,
  whether or not they hire us.
- No accuracy figure may be stated as measured. No charter price may be stated as
  fact. No claim that this business is overwhelmed or misses calls, the evidence
  is about a message channel and it says channel, never strain.
