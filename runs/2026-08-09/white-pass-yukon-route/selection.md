## TODAY'S PICK

**White Pass & Yukon Route Railway**, wpyr.com, Skagway (plus Carcross and
Whitehorse, Yukon). Segment tourism. **fit_total 23**, the highest of the 24.

Scores: ai_solvable_pain 4, ability_to_pay 5, reachability 5, offer_fit 4,
alaska_signal 5.

Why it won. It is the only candidate that scored 5 on both ability to pay and
reachability. A 1898 gold-rush railway that is a National Historic Civil
Engineering Landmark, running excursion volume against the cruise dock at Skagway,
with a named president and a named HR and payroll lead and a published business
address on its own contact page. Its front line is almost entirely seasonal,
ticket agents, dock representatives and brakemen, and we are sitting three to four
weeks from the end of the season, which is when that workforce leaves and the
operating knowledge leaves with it.

OWNERSHIP CHECKED BEFORE LOCKING, because the scout scored alaska_signal 5 and the
ICP disqualifies a national chain with an Alaska pin on a map. White Pass was sold
in August 2018 for 290 million dollars to Klondike Holdings, LLC. The majority
partner is Survey Point Holdings, an Alaska company based in Ketchikan. Carnival
plc holds a MINORITY interest through Holland America Princess Alaska Tours. So it
is an Alaska railroad majority-owned by an Alaska company, not a chain outpost, and
the score stands. The research room re-verifies this from the company's own pages
before anything ships.
