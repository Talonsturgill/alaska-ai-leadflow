# Koniag, Incorporated, disqualified at the Phase 2 research gate

**Verdict: values. Suppressed, never contacted, no email drafted.**

## What happened

Koniag led the merged Phase 1 shortlist at 23, the highest of twenty candidates,
and it deserved to. Real scale, a verified named contact read in clear, and the
sharpest timing in the state, a federal-services arm looking straight at a
September 30 fiscal year end.

The company-analyst's adversarial reputation check turned up a single
trade-aggregator report that on 2026-07-29, six days before this run, a Koniag
subsidiary had won a $63,035,230.95 task order supporting ICE Enforcement and
Removal Operations. The analyst flagged it honestly as UNVERIFIED, said it could
not be confirmed on any .gov page, and noted a contractor-profile site that
showed no such award, most likely a data lag.

That was the right way to hand it over, and it was not enough to act on. A
suppression is a durable claim about a real company, and the aggregator that
first surfaced it is the same class of source that, earlier in this same run,
got Chenega wrong twice.

So it was checked against the primary source. USASpending returns:

```
17456917.40 | 70CDCR26FR0000034 | KONIAG SERVICES, INC.
             | U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement
             | THE PURPOSE OF THIS TASK ORDER IS TO PROVIDE SUPPORT SERVICES
               IN SUPPORT OF ENFORCEMENT AND REMOVAL OPERATIONS
```

The award ID matches the report exactly, the recipient matches, the awarding
sub-agency is ICE, and the description is ERO support. The dollar figure differs
because USASpending shows obligated-to-date against a four-year ceiling, which is
normal and not a discrepancy. The award is real.

## Why that disqualifies

Four Alaska Native corporations are already suppressed in this ledger on exactly
this rule, Bering Straits, NANA, Ahtna and Doyon. Every one of them for ICE work.

The Bering Straits reasoning applies here with more force than it did there. What
we would have offered Koniag is AI for its federal capture and proposal pipeline.
That build does not sit next to the ICE work, it accelerates it. We would have
been proposing, for free, to make this company faster at winning the exact
category of contract that got four of its peers suppressed.

There is no version of this study that is honest and also comfortable. Saying the
honest thing is supposed to BE the pitch, and the honest thing here is that we
should not be helping.

## What was NOT the basis

Koniag Brillient LLC holds a large USCIS records-operations contract. That is
immigration benefits processing, not enforcement, and it was not part of this
decision. Recording that distinction so a future run does not widen the rule
past what it says.

Also not the basis, though both were found and both are real:

- Koniag already sells AI. It owns Koniag AI Solutions, hired an SVP of Strategic
  Intelligence in October 2025 to accelerate AI, and publicly claims a live Army
  automation saving more than $37 million a year. That made it a HARDER company to
  write an honest study for, not a disqualifying one, and the competitor room had
  found a genuinely good and non-hypey angle before the values check landed.
- A 2025 GAO bid protest it lost, and mixed employee reviews. Both routine.

## The replacement, and the lesson applied immediately

Next name on the queue is Tyonek Native Corporation at 22. Before spending a
second research room on it, the same USASpending screen was run against it rather
than waiting for a research agent to surface the problem at the gate again.

Tyonek's top 100 awards: 89 Department of Defense (Army 33, Navy 26, Air Force
21, DCMA 9), 5 U.S. Customs and Border Protection, 2 GSA, 2 SOCOM, 1 DTRA, 1
Coast Guard. **Zero ICE. Zero detention. Zero removal.**

The five CBP rows are Tyonek Aircraft Overhaul doing radar and aerostat
sustainment, TARS sites, maritime approaches surveillance towers, a radar
purchase. That is border surveillance hardware maintained by an aircraft-overhaul
company, which is its actual trade. It is materially different from detaining or
removing people, and the ledger's rule is about the latter.

That line is worth stating plainly rather than quietly: this run drew the
boundary at ICE, the agency that detains and removes people, and did not extend
it to every DHS component. Flagging it in the delivery summary so Talon can move
the line if he wants it somewhere else. It is his brand and his call, and a
routine running unattended should not widen a values rule on its own initiative.

## Cost of the disqualification

One research room, roughly one third of the run's agent budget. The run continues
and ships. A disqualified candidate is never the end of a run.
