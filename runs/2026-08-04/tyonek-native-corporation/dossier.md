# Internal dossier, Tyonek Native Corporation, 2026-08-04

PRIVATE. Never published. The public package is the study page and the demo only.

## The run in one paragraph

Four scouts returned twenty candidates. Koniag, Incorporated led at 23 and was
disqualified at the Phase 2 research gate on ICE Enforcement and Removal
Operations contracting, confirmed against USASpending rather than the trade
aggregator that first reported it. Tyonek Native Corporation, next on the queue at
22, was screened the same way BEFORE a second research room was spent on it. The
study went six rounds with the study-critic and three with the fact-checker. The
email went four with the lead-critic. Delivered.

## The pick

**Affiliate Past Performance Completeness Check**, downscoped by the
ai-feasibility-engineer from the discovery room's larger "Documentation Engine".

Retrieval over a permissioned set of their executed agreements, a deterministic
completeness rule over an entity register keyed on UEI and CAGE, and two bounded
model calls, neither of which can move the go/no-go verdict. No agent anywhere.

The ASK is not the build. It is a half-day baseline retrospective whose honest
return is decision data, carrying a written kill condition we commit to before
taking any money.

## What the numbers actually said

The half day returns $2.75 for every dollar in the conservative case. The build
recovers 4 percent of its five-year cost conservatively and **pays back in cash
NEVER in any of the three scenarios**. That asymmetry is the study's headline and
its heading, not a footnote.

Splitting freed hours out of the cash return, which the critic caught as a direct
hit on the ROI_METHOD hype table, is what produced that result. It made our own
recommendation weaker and the study far better.

## The constraint that governed every sentence

NOTHING in the verified claims shows Tyonek has ever failed to document an
affiliate. GAO dismissed Tyonek Technical Innovations' protest on untimeliness
BECAUSE the provision was "unambiguously clear" on its face, and they protested
it, which is evidence of attention rather than of fumbling.

So the study argues from the SIZE and IRREVERSIBILITY of the exposure across 22
registrations, never from an implied failure. The fact-checker had to enforce this
twice, and the second time it caught the study asserting that no business
development leader appeared in Tyonek's public materials while citing the page
that names Joe Webb, Vice President of Business Development.

## Killed in the open

- **Quality records (AS9102, NCRs)** on ITAR, not the FAA rules. The engineer
  corrected the discovery room on which law does the killing, and it matters
  because it tells them which door reopens under which condition.
- **Courseware** on an unknown classification, not carried as a costed item at all.
- **Recruiting** on commodity economics. Their ATS vendor probably ships it.
- **Predictive maintenance** on the GAO and DoD Inspector General evidence.
  Tyonek is not behind here, it is in the majority.
