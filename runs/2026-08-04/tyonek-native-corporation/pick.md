# LOCKED PICK, 2026-08-04, Tyonek Native Corporation

## The build

**Affiliate Past Performance Completeness Check**, downscoped by the
ai-feasibility-engineer from the discovery room's "Documentation Engine".

Retrieval over a permissioned corpus, a deterministic completeness rule set over
an entity registry keyed on UEI and CAGE, and two bounded model calls, neither of
which can move the go/no-go verdict. No agent anywhere.

## What the conscience cut, and why it matters

The feasibility gate was told to try to kill the pick. It nearly did, and what
survived is smaller and more honest than what went in.

1. **The word Engine.** It produces a gap list and a draft a human rewrites and
   owns. It does not produce the proposal section. The noun should be the thing
   it actually does.
2. **The model, out of the verdict path.** The completeness decision is a set
   difference over a registry keyed on UEI and CAGE, evaluated by rules that can
   be tested exhaustively. The model does roughly a quarter of the work and zero
   percent of the go/no-go. The study says that out loud.
3. **The significant-and-critical determination.** That is a contractual
   representation about future work share. A human at Tyonek decides it. The
   model assembles prose from approved facts and a human approves the prose.
4. **Solicitation extraction, from replacement to redundancy.** A capture manager
   reads Section L and fills a form in ten minutes. The defensible value is a
   second independent read whose DISAGREEMENT raises a flag.
5. **Corpus assembly moved INTO scope.** Signed affiliation, teaming and JV
   agreements are executed PDFs with scanned signature pages spread across
   Anchorage, Madison, Huntsville, Warner Robins and Kiln. Assembling and
   normalizing that is phase one, it is real work, and it is NOT AI work.
   Pricing it as a customer precondition would be hiding the project inside an
   assumption.

## THE FINDING THAT ALMOST KILLED IT, and which now governs the whole study

GAO dismissed ground one on untimeliness **because** "the plain language of the
solicitation made it unambiguously clear how the agency intended to treat the
past performance information of affiliated companies."

A requirement GAO calls unambiguously clear on its face is one a competent
capture team can read and comply with. And Tyonek PROTESTED that provision, which
is evidence they were paying close attention to it, not evidence they were
fumbling it.

**NOTHING in the verified claims shows Tyonek has ever failed to document an
affiliate.** The study argues from the SIZE and IRREVERSIBILITY of the exposure
across 22 registrations. It never argues from an implied failure. That is the
single easiest place for this study to become dishonest and it is now a standing
constraint on every downstream room.

Honest confidence is 0.4 to 0.5, not the 0.7 discovery carried.

## Cagan's four risks, checked before locking

- **Feasibility.** Done by the engineer. Survives, downscoped.
- **Value.** Conditional and honestly so. The exposure is real and total. Whether
  it is currently being realised is UNMEASURED, which is why the ask is what it is.
- **Usability.** It attaches to a rhythm capture teams already have, as a
  completeness check on documents they already produce. It replaces no process
  anyone is loyal to.
- **Business viability.** $341,146,301 in 2024 revenue. Ability to pay is not in
  question. The risk is not money, it is that no capture or BD leader is named
  anywhere in the verified claims, and AI_SCOPING puts sustained ownership among
  the few things separating projects that work from those that do not. If no
  owner emerges in the first conversation, that alone is grounds not to build.

## ECONOMICS PRE-CHECK, and the ask this study will actually make

The ask is sized HERE, before the engineering room spends, and the roi-analyst
builds the case for THIS ask and not for the biggest build we can imagine.

**THE ASK IS THE HALF-DAY RETROSPECTIVE, NOT THE BUILD.**

A half-day review of their last five to ten completed bids that named affiliate
past performance, using documents they already hold, requiring zero new
instrumentation. Four numbers come out of it:

- affiliates named per bid versus affiliates whose full required documentation
  set actually shipped, readable straight off the submitted proposal
- capture hours spent assembling that documentation per bid
- count of qualifying bids in the last twelve months, which is the reach
  denominator the entire case rests on and is currently an assumption
- whether any debrief has ever noted an affiliate reference not evaluated

**The honest return on that ask is DECISION DATA**, and ROI_METHOD explicitly
allows a pilot priced as decision data so long as it is stated in those terms.
It is stated in those terms.

**THE WRITTEN KILL CONDITION**, in the study, before anyone takes any money.
If measured completeness is above roughly 90 percent AND median assembly time is
under roughly a day per bid AND there are fewer than roughly eight qualifying
bids a year, we say so in writing and we do not build this.

Putting a named kill condition on our own recommendation, in the document that
proposes it, is the trust move. It costs half a day to find out whether we
deserve the engagement.

This clears the conservative bar by construction, because the deliverable of the
ask is a measurement whose value does not depend on the measurement's outcome.
The Phase 1 build is priced in the study as a GATED next step, contingent on the
baseline, never as the thing being bought today.

The fallback is the layer UNDERNEATH the pick, which survives independently.

## Killed, in the open

- **Quality Records Assistant.** ITAR, not the FAA rules. Discovery misattributed
  this and the correction matters, because it tells Tyonek which door reopens
  under which condition. AS9102 FAI content is drawing-derived technical data,
  and 22 CFR 120.54(b)(1) grants the encryption carve-out only where the data is
  never in unencrypted form and the means of decryption go to no third party.
  Hosted inference needs plaintext at the provider, so the carve-out is LOST, not
  strained. Re-enterable only self-hosted inside their own environment.
- **Courseware Production Assistant.** Best pure language fit in the entire map,
  which is exactly why the no is credible. The classification of 39th IOS
  courseware is unknown and an unknown handling level in a defense training
  environment is a stop, not a risk to price. Not carried as a costed roadmap
  item at all.
- **Recruiting Assistant.** Killed on commodity economics rather than on
  Tyonek. Their ATS vendor probably already ships this. Recommending a custom
  build would be selling effort we know is worse value than a purchase.
- **Predictive Maintenance.** Killed on the GAO and DoD Inspector General
  evidence. Tyonek is not behind here, it is in the majority.

Two of six killed on regulation, one on commodity economics, one on evidence. A
proposal that got smaller under examination is travelling in the right direction
and the study says so plainly rather than smoothing it over.

## The where-we-would-not-use-AI line, which ships

The completeness verdict itself, because a rule does it, gives the same answer
every time, and can be tested exhaustively. With four registrations beginning
TFAB Ground Systems and three named Tyonek Engineering and Agile Manufacturing
across three states, a fuzzy name match that is right most of the time is the
exact thing that loses a bid, so nothing here ever matches on a name.
The significant-and-critical determination, because it is a contractual
representation and a human makes it. Anything touching a maintenance sign-off or
return to service, because 14 CFR 145.213(d) and 43.9(a)(4) reserve that to a
certificated person and we would not touch it even if they did not. Government
property records, because they are structured, rule-governed and high-risk, and
they posted an analyst to own them.
