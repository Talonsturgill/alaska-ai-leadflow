## Discovery and feasibility

The strategist mapped the whole business, then DECLINED the two highest-scoring
opportunities on purpose: the front-desk phone (Ulwick 15) on adoptability and
absent evidence, and online self-scheduling (14) because it is not an AI problem
and a verified local peer already runs it.

The feasibility engineer then INVERTED the pick. Its deliberately AI-free control
candidate out-scored the AI one on RICE, 1.36 to 1.23, and the honest response was
to ship it as a real deliverable rather than reframe it as scaffolding. It killed
the AI history summary, killed the voice agent on step-count arithmetic (ten
judgement steps, 35 to 60 percent end-to-end), and reclassified the athenahealth
write-back path from a confidence discount to a gating dependency.

## What we declined, and it is the point

- An ambient scribe, from us or anyone, because athenahealth's chief executive
  said on record it goes into the core athenaOne fee at no incremental charge.
  We did NOT claim scribes do not work, the randomised evidence is positive.
- Prior authorization, the LARGEST claim available at 45 percent of practice
  phone time, because not one of their pages mentions it and we will not use a
  national average as their baseline.
- Anything on the phones, the after-hours path, or the coding stack.
