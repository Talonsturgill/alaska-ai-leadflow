# Timely Alaska context, 2026-08-05

- Peak visitor season, last full month. Cruise season runs into late September but
  the labor cliff is late August, when J-1 and seasonal staff leave and operators
  run the shoulder on a skeleton crew.
- Salmon. Bristol Bay sockeye is effectively over, Southeast pinks and Kenai silvers
  are running now. Processors are in the back half of the season.
- Fire season winding down, freeze-up planning starts in the Interior and on the
  North Slope.
- Legislature out of session. State FY2027 is a month in, so agency and municipal
  procurement calendars are opening.
- PFD applications closed 31 March, distribution is early October. Retail and
  services plan around it.
- Alaska State Fair opens late August in Palmer.
- Federal fiscal year end is 30 September, so federal contracting shops are in
  their heaviest proposal and award window right now.
