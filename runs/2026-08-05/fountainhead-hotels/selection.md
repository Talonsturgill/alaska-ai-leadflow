## The pick

**Fountainhead Hotels (Fountainhead Development, Inc.), Fairbanks. Total 24.**

fountainheadhotels.com

Three candidates tied at 24. The contract breaks a tie on (a) reachability first,
then (b) offer_fit, then (c) a segment we already have proof in. Fountainhead is
the only one of the three scoring a 5 on reachability, so it wins on the first
tiebreak and the others are not reached.

What reachability 5 rests on. A named human with a published business email
rendered as a live mailto on a page the scout actually fetched, Becky Kunkle,
Extended Stay and Reservations Manager, beckyk@fdialaska.com, plus a second
published address, jobs@fdifairbanks.com. Note both sit on Fountainhead
Development domains rather than the hotel-brand domain, which the research room
must confirm rather than assume. No address gets used until the fact-checker sees
it on a page it fetched itself.

Why it is a real business and not a chain with a pin. Family-held Fairbanks
group running Wedgewood Resort (305 rooms on 105 acres, with the Fountainhead
Antique Auto Museum and a wildlife sanctuary), Sophie Station Suites with Zach's
Restaurant, and the seasonal Bear Lodge with the Golden Bear Restaurant.
Independently confirmed operating by Explore Fairbanks.

The pain the scout saw on their own pages, quoted. The jobs page states "We more
than double our current hotel staffing for summer to make sure guests have
caring, personal experiences." The seasonal roster it must refill and then lose
includes Front Desk Clerks, Group Representatives, Bell Captains, Porters,
Shuttle Bus Drivers and a full food and beverage crew. Bear Lodge front desk is
listed as reachable only May through August, so a whole property's phone coverage
disappears at the labor cliff. Today is 5 August, which puts that cliff about
three weeks out.

The honest caveat carried forward. Offer fit was scored 4, not 5, because hotel
reservations sit inside a property management system and a channel manager, so
anything touching the booking path has integration friction that a tour operator
does not have. That is a note for the discovery room, not a conclusion. The room
maps the whole business first and the front desk is only one part of it, there is
also a museum, a wildlife sanctuary, restaurants, catering and convention
business. The revenue figure floating in third-party aggregators was never
verified on a real page and must not be cited.

has already suppressed Bering Straits and NANA for ICE and DHS detention
contracting and Alutiiq for federal-contracting fraud history. It cleared all six
of its candidates on detention contracting, False Claims Act matters,
sham-subsidiary allegations and kickback matters, while flagging honestly that
for Aleut and Old Harbor this was an absence of coverage rather than a completed
screen. Those two carry that caveat into any future run that reaches them.

Corporation is an Alutiiq-culture village corporation and is NOT Alutiiq, LLC,
the suppressed Afognak subsidiary. Different companies, different owners. The
shared cultural term must never read as a suppressed match.
