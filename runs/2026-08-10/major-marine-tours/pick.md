# THE LOCKED PICK, 2026-08-10

## What is locked

**The Reservations Desk Answer Layer.** One funded build with **no AI in it**, plus a
written, numerically gated **option** on snippet retrieval.

The locked build is the ai-feasibility-engineer's `recommended_pick`, which is the
strategist's provisional pick after the conscience gate cut it down. The gate
changed it in seven ways and every change made it smaller and more honest.

### The funded scope

1. **A paper tally sheet on the desk, starting now.** Nine weeks of the 2026 season
   remain. Question asked, time to answer, escalated or not, to whom. Zero software,
   zero cost. It does three jobs at once: it creates the baseline that does not
   exist, it counts the disruption events that decide whether anything else is ever
   worth building, and it starts the miss log before a tool exists.
2. **Corpus reconciliation.** One canonical, dated, owned policy source. This is the
   largest item of hidden scope in the original proposal and it was not costed. It is
   content operations, not modelling, and it needs a named internal owner because only
   they know which parking price is the right one.
3. **The authoritative vessel-to-departure map.** This data does not exist in verified
   published form and, on the evidence, may not exist internally in one place either.
   Named owner, named date, and an abstain route if it turns out not to be stable.
4. **The decision table**, over the four highest-cost-of-error branches: refund clocks
   and change fees, accessibility by vessel, shuttle eligibility, dietary windows.
   Exhaustively tested against every input combination. Returns the verbatim published
   sentence for the agent to read.
5. **The miss log**, specified properly: verbatim questions, labelled answers from a
   named internal authority, a held-out split, deliberate out-of-corpus cases, a
   supervisor-logged sample to correct its own selection bias, and a PII rule agreed
   before the first entry.

### The option, not a phase

Snippet retrieval over the reconciled corpus, staff-facing only, verbatim source
mandatory, no composed prose. It is **priced and gated on a number that can fail**:
at least 15 percent of logged questions falling outside the table, AND at least 20
distinct question types recurring three or more times, AND at least 150 labelled
items after de-duplication. If the log lands below that, we say so in writing, we do
not invoice, and the published conclusion is that this business needed a rule set
and not an AI.

**Timeline, corrected by the gate.** Phase one is in the March 2027 cohort's hands.
The retrieval option, if it is ever exercised, is a **2028** tool, because the
questions that would justify it can only be collected while a season runs. The
strategist dated both against one March 2027 clock, which quietly compressed a
two-season programme into one.

## Cagan's four risks, checked before locking

**Feasibility.** Retired by the ai-feasibility-engineer. The funded build is a
deterministic decision table with a finite, published input space. It is the only
component in the whole proposal whose acceptance bar can be *certified* rather than
sampled: 100 percent agreement across an exhaustive enumeration, not a threshold on a
sample. Zero hallucination surface.

**Value.** Strong and evidenced. The target population is a workforce that is
structurally rebuilt every spring by the company's own published hiring facts, about
60 seasonal staff hired January 15th to April 15th including Customer Service and
Sales Staff. The strongest citation available measures its largest effect on exactly
that population and almost none on veterans. Habit is near zero for a March hire,
which is the rarest and most valuable adoption condition in the whole analysis.

**Usability.** A lookup that answers four or five structured questions and returns a
date, a fee, an eligibility and the published sentence to read aloud. A first-week
hire can use it in their first hour. The design explicitly does *not* target the
veterans, and asking Colby Lawrence at 22 years or Rodney Roemmich at 15 to adopt it
would manufacture a failure.

**Business viability. THIS IS THE ONE THAT NEEDED WORK AND IT PASSES, NARROWLY.**
Ability to pay is real, the owner runs Major Marine plus two hotels plus a Hertz
franchise and sits on a bank board. Two viability risks are genuine and neither is
disqualifying:
- **The adoption dependency is on them, not us.** The tally sheet, the corpus owner
  and the vessel map are all *their* work. If nobody at Major Marine will run a paper
  tally for nine weeks, the outcome is unmeasurable and the study must say so rather
  than promise a measured result. That is stated in the plan, not hidden.
- **The prize is modest in absolute terms** because the desk is small and its headcount
  is unpublished. This is a ramp-time and error-rate case that recurs every spring, not
  a headcount case, and the ask has to be sized to match. Which brings us to:

## ECONOMICS PRE-CHECK, before the engineering room spends

**The question: what is the SMALLEST honest ask this build supports, and does its
conservative case clear its own bar?**

**The ask is sized here at 18,000 dollars, fixed price, for phase one.** The
engineering room builds the case for *that* ask, not for the biggest build we can
imagine.

### Why 18,000 and not more

Roughly one person-month to build and exhaustively test the decision table, plus
about half a month of content-operations work on corpus reconciliation and
facilitating the vessel map, plus the miss-log tooling. A larger number is not
defensible on this scope, and a larger number is what would break the conservative
case.

### The back-of-envelope, and the honest problem in it

There is **no baseline**. Median time to answer, escalation rate and misquote count
are recorded nowhere today. So a hard-dollar benefit strand can be *modelled* but not
*measured*, and every driver in it is an assumption until the tally sheet runs.

Two conservative hard-dollar strands survive scrutiny:
- **Refund-clock errors in the generous direction.** Quoting the 72-hour cruise-only
  clock to a guest holding a 21-day transportation package means refunding money the
  company did not owe, on a booking whose third-party transportation it may already be
  committed to. That is a real cash loss and it is the one error direction that costs
  the company rather than the guest.
- **First-season ramp time.** Hours a new agent spends finding an answer, or waiting
  on a veteran, in weeks one through six.

One strand that looks like money and **is not**, and the ROI analyst must not use it:
a wrong shuttle answer that causes a missed sailing produces a **no-show**, which the
published policy makes completely non-refundable. The company *keeps* that fare. The
cost there is goodwill and reputation, not revenue, and dressing it as revenue would
be exactly the drift this run has already had to correct twice.

### The verdict

**The conservative case clears at 18,000, and it clears on a mix rather than on hours
alone.** The honest composition is a modest error-avoidance strand, a modest ramp
strand, and **decision data plus three retained assets** — a canonical policy source,
an authoritative vessel map, and a written record of what their desk actually gets
asked. The contract explicitly permits a pilot whose honest return is decision data
that gates a bigger spend, and that is a fair description of a large part of this one.

**The gate does not fire.** There is an honest ask shape at a size this build
supports. But the margin is thin, and that has three binding consequences for Phase 4:

1. **The roi-analyst must return every driver as an explicit number** and the
   showrunner computes every derived figure in `roi_math.py`. No narrated arithmetic.
2. **If the computed conservative case does not clear, the ROI section says so in its
   heading**, per FIELD_STUDY_SPEC. It does not get buried in a footnote and the ask
   does not get quietly inflated to make it work.
3. **No hours-saved case may be modelled on an unpublished headcount.** Any hours
   figure before the tally sheet runs is an invented denominator.

## What rides to the roadmap's Later lane

Nothing found is wasted. In the delivery-lead's priority order:
1. The guest-facing chatbot, gated on a full season of internal evidence.
2. The disruption batch mode, gated on a counted event frequency.
3. The package configurator, gated on a conversation about whether phone-only is a
   deliberate sales decision.
4. Seat allocation, gated on any evidence at all that it currently hurts.

## What is dead and stays dead

The voice agent (kill confirmed, on durable grounds rather than on latency), free-text
generation on the four high-cost branches, a model drafting disruption notices, an
"AI website consistency checker", a seat-allocation optimiser, and review-response
tooling. The last of those is a **gift**: a 61,459-call audit measured review responses
at +0.1 percentage points on how models recommend, statistically zero. We hand that
over with no invoice attached.

# ASK RESIZED AT PHASE 4. THE ECONOMICS PRE-CHECK WAS WRONG.

**The conservative case does NOT clear at 18,000 dollars, and the roi-analyst said
so in its heading rather than in a footnote.** Computed in `scripts/roi_math.py`,
not narrated:

| Scenario | Ask | 5yr benefit | 5yr TCO | Recovered | Payback |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative | 18,000 | 16,320 | 51,720 | **32 percent** | **none in 5 years** |
| Most likely | 18,000 | 57,135 | 45,784 | 125 percent | month 45 |
| Aggressive | 18,000 | 158,796 | 42,320 | 375 percent | month 16 |
| **Phase Zero, conservative** | **4,500** | **13,991** | **13,320** | **105 percent** | **month 56** |

**THE ASK IS NOW 4,500 DOLLARS FOR A PHASE ZERO WITH NO SOFTWARE IN IT.** That is
the only version of this engagement whose conservative case clears, and it clears
thinly at 105 percent of five-year TCO.

## What Phase Zero is

The corpus crawl and the conflict list. Facilitated adjudication with a named
internal owner, producing one dated canonical policy source. The vessel-map
workshop with a written abstain rule. The paper tally sheet installed for the final
nine weeks of the 2026 season. The miss-log specification, including the PII rule,
the held-out split and the supervisor sampling arm.

No decision table. No software of any kind.

## What happens to the 18,000 dollar build

It is **not withdrawn, it is re-priced against a measured baseline in autumn 2027**,
when the tally sheet has produced the denominator this analysis never had. If the
counted error frequency lands anywhere near the most-likely assumption, the build
clears conservatively at that point and we show the arithmetic again with real
numbers in it.

## The disclosure that has to travel with this

The analyst volunteered it and it stays in: **the 4,500 figure was arrived at by
solving for the ceiling the conservative benefit supports, and the scope was then
cut to fit under it.** That is a legitimate way to size an ask and it is also
exactly the move Flyvbjerg warns about, numbers reverse-engineered to clear
approval. So it is disclosed rather than presented as a coincidence. If the scope
can't honestly be delivered for 4,500, the ask does not go up, the recommendation
becomes the free tally sheet alone.

## Why this is the right outcome rather than a failure

The no-pay gate did not fire, because an honest ask shape exists at a size this
build supports. What changed is that the room found the honest size and it was a
quarter of what the showrunner guessed. A study that recommends a 4,500 dollar
engagement, tells the prospect the 18,000 dollar version can't be justified until
they have counted something, and hands them a free tally sheet either way, is the
whole thesis of this desk working as designed.

The conservative case at 18,000 failing is not buried. It appears in the ROI
section's heading, per FIELD_STUDY_SPEC.
