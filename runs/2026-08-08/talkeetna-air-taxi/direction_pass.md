One question, asked of every sentence that asserts something unflattering about
them. Does this make them look worse than the evidence actually does?

This run had an unusual amount of warning. The fact-checker returned a
`drift_pattern` naming seven rejections that all leaned the same way, so the
pass below is checking whether the study inherited a lean the claims file had
already been scrubbed of.

## THE THREE THAT DID NOT SURVIVE

**7. "avoidable calls" becomes "day-of calls".** Could we know it from outside
their building? No. We know their published FAQ directs the call, and we know
nobody has counted them. Whether any of those calls are avoidable is precisely
what the counted week exists to find out, so asserting it in a heading is a
guess wearing a finding's clothes, and it is a guess that flatters our own
build. The heading already carries "nobody has counted them" in the same
breath, which is what made it easy to miss.

**8. The K2 row asserted a guest behaviour we cannot see.** The verified facts
are that their webcam URL 301s and that K2 carries a live camera link on its
FAQ. That a guest goes and looks at K2's instead is inference, and it is the
most damaging inference in the document because it implies leakage to a
competitor. Rewritten to state the fact, that the nearest live camera link on a
Talkeetna operator's site is K2's, and to drop the claim about what the guest
then does.

**10. The price comparison in WHAT IT IS COSTING was decoration.** It restates
`found.body_2` and adds a competitor framing that makes the same fact sting
more without carrying any new argument. It fails the second test outright, the
unflattering reading is not load-bearing there, it just makes the pitch easier.
Cut entirely.

## THE ACCUMULATION CHECK, WHICH IS THE HALF A REGEX CANNOT SEE

The "your pages disagree" finding appears in the thesis, the brief, the WHAT WE
FOUND heading, `found.body_2`, the roadmap and the metrics. Six places for one
unflattering fact is the pattern this pass exists to catch.

It survives, narrowly, and the reason is that it is genuinely load-bearing in
five of those six. It is the verified baseline, it is the entire justification
for the Facts Spine, it is the one metric in the study with a baseline we did
not have to ask them for, and it is the prerequisite that gates the AI phase.
Cutting item 10 removes the one appearance that was carrying no argument, which
brings it to five and each of those five is doing work.

## THE COUNTERWEIGHTS, CHECKED FOR PRESENCE

The fact-checker named two facts that cut in their favour and asked that they be
carried. Both are in the study.

- One of only four federal Denali glacier landing concessions, 2020 to 2029. In
  the sources and referenced in the analysis.
- An unconditional 100 percent refund, more generous than either published
  competitor policy. Carried in the brief, in the honest section, and used as
  the reason the expensive error direction runs opposite to instinct.

A third counterweight is structural rather than a fact, and it is present
throughout: the manual parts of this operation are read as DELIBERATE. The
check-in briefing is called the product rather than the bottleneck, the decision
handed to the customer is protected rather than solved, and the study says
plainly that automating it would be value destruction. That framing is what
keeps the whole document on the right side of the nod test.

## WOULD THEY RECOGNISE THEMSELVES

Yes, after the three fixes. The opening fact is something an owner already knows
is true, since he wrote the policy. The webcam being dead is a thing he can
check in ten seconds. The three hours answers are his own pages. None of the
three requires him to accept a characterisation of his business he would
dispute, and the document tells him twice not to buy the thing we are selling if
his own count comes back small.
