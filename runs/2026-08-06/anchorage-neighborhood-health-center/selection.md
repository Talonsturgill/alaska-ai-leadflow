# Selection, 2026-08-06

Four lead-scouts ran in parallel, one per ICP segment. 24 candidates came back,
all 24 cleared `ledger.py check` against the git ledger, so nothing on this list
was ever contacted or suppressed. Two ANC candidates were screened out on values
before scoring and are now suppressed (thtbc.com, kikiktagruk.com).

## THE PICK

**Anchorage Neighborhood Health Center (anhc.org), Anchorage. Fit total 24.**

Highest total on the merged shortlist, and it won outright so no tiebreak was
needed. It scored 5 on ai_solvable_pain, reachability, offer_fit and
alaska_signal, and 4 on ability_to_pay. It is also the least-covered segment we
target: the ledger holds 6 tourism leads, 6 ANC, 4 other, and exactly ONE
healthcare lead, which is the segment config/icp.yaml calls the highest-ROI use
anywhere and the largest private industry in the state.

## SCREENED OUT ON VALUES BEFORE SCORING

- **Tlingit Haida Tribal Business Corporation (thtbc.com)**. Subsidiary KIRA holds
  a US Navy port operations contract at Guantanamo Bay to February 2028 at a base
  being used to hold migrants, and THTBC had to publicly deny BOP, INS and ICE
  contracts after its own tribal citizens raised concerns. Suppressed, never contacted.
- **Kikiktagruk Inupiat Corporation (kikiktagruk.com)**. Paid 2,025,000 dollars in
  May 2019 to settle False Claims Act allegations over kickbacks steering a Fort
  Bliss set-aside contract, two employees served five-year sentences. Directly on
  point with the proposal and compliance work we would propose. Suppressed, never contacted.
