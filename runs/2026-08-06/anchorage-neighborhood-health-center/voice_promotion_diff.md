**Where.** Append to `## The CTA (small and reply-first)`, after the bullets and
before the "NOTHING IS GATED" paragraph.

**Add this.**

```
THE NO STRINGS CLAUSE IS PART OF THE CTA (promoted from the deltas, 2026-08-06).
Four separate sends went out with a no-pressure clause the routine never drafted,
and it is the single most repeated hand edit on record. The drafted CTA reads as
an ask. The sent one reads as a gift with an ask attached, which is the register

be added.

One short clause, inside the same sentence, flat and unadorned. It never becomes
a second sentence about how relaxed we are, because a paragraph explaining that
nothing is expected is itself a form of pressure.
- "Worth a reply either way, no strings."
- "No strings, tell us if it's useful."

```

**Cost.** Two words. A sixty word body has room, and if it does not, the cut
comes from sentence two.

## DIFF 2, name the deliverable as theirs

**Where.** Replace item 3 of `## Shape to aim for`.

**Evidence, 4 separate sends.**

| Date | Drafted | Sent |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-08-04, Tyonek | We've already done the study on how to measure that exposure. | We just wrapped up our study on how to measure that exposure and did a deep dive on Tyonek. |
| 2026-07-31, Calista | We already did the study on closing that list and the first thing we'd build has no AI in it. | We already did the week long study on closing that list and the first thing we'd build is attached along with our deep dive into your business: |

| 2026-07-29, Allen Marine | If it is yours, reply yes and I will take it from there. | If it is yours, click the link to see our full review and let us know what you think if you'd like. |

Every one of those edits does the same thing. The draft says "the study" and he
changes it to a deep dive **on Tyonek**, a field study **into your company**, our
full review. The abstract noun becomes a possessive one. He is answering the
question the reader actually has, which is not what did you write, it is who is
it about.

**Replace.**

```
3. The link, named plainly as work already done, ABOUT THEM. Not "the study".
   Talon has rewritten that exact abstraction by hand on four separate sends,
   every time into something possessive, a deep dive on Tyonek, our field study
   into your company, our full review. The reader's question is not what did you
   write, it is who is it about, so the sentence answers that before they click.
```

**Cost.** Two or three words, and it buys the click.
