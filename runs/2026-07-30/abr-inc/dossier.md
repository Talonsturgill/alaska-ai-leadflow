## The finding
ABR names machine learning on two of its own service pages and its homepage says only
"leading edge technologies". A positioning gap, not a capability gap. They already run ML,
build databases and mobile apps in house, and ship software to the USGS Alaska Science Center.

## The build, and why we are NOT selling it
Locked pick: a cited search and assembly tool over an audited corpus of ABR's own approved
proposal text, deterministic search shipped first as the baseline any model layer must beat.
THE CONSERVATIVE CASE DOES NOT CLEAR. Break-even needs about 31 past-performance-bearing
submissions a year, which for a 36-person firm is one every 1.7 weeks. The study says so in its
section heading and asks ABR to COUNT rather than to buy. Two things ship free: a drafted
homepage paragraph and a post-season data validation rule list.
